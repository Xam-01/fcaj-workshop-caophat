# FCAJ & Agentic AI Build Week

### Event Objectives

- Introduced the latest trends in Agentic AI and AI Native applications on AWS, highlighting how these technologies are being used to build intelligent, autonomous systems for real-world business scenarios.
- Shared practical experience in developing, deploying, and presenting AI-powered solutions, covering the entire product lifecycle from idea generation and solution architecture design to delivering a functional product within a limited timeframe during Agentic AI Build Week and Hackathon events.


### Speakers

- **Team OneTeam**
- **Team Signal Scout** 
- **Team Plan V** 
- **Team 3KA** 
- **Team Six Pillars** 

### Key Highlights

- **AI Native Projects on AWS**: The event brought together development teams participating in Agentic AI Build Week, showcasing a variety of AI Native solutions built on AWS. Featured projects included AI-powered data management and analytics platforms, an intelligent assistant for Solutions Architects to design cloud architectures, a smart surveillance system leveraging computer vision and AI Agents, and an anti-money laundering solution for the financial sector. The speakers walked the audience through the entire product development lifecycle, from problem identification and system architecture design to selecting appropriate AWS services, deployment, and real-world operations.
  
- **Hackathon Experience**: The participating teams shared their journey of building products within the limited timeframe of a Hackathon, covering idea generation, task allocation, MVP development, technical problem-solving, and presentation preparation. These real-world experiences highlighted the importance of product thinking, effective teamwork, time management, and leveraging AI to accelerate the software development process.


### Key Takeaways

- **Cloud and System Design Mindset:** Through the presentations, I realized that developing an AI-powered product requires not only strong programming skills but also a solid foundation in Cloud Computing and the ability to design scalable, maintainable system architectures that can support long-term business growth.

- **System Security:** I gained a deeper understanding of the importance of **multi-agent systems**, agent memory management, and implementing security mechanisms such as **AWS IAM** and **AWS KMS** when deploying AI solutions in enterprise environments. These components play a critical role in protecting data, controlling access, and ensuring the secure operation of AI applications.

- **Deeper Understanding of AI Agents:** The event provided valuable insights into how **Agentic AI** systems operate by coordinating multiple AI agents, managing shared memory, and integrating external tools and data sources to improve reasoning and decision-making capabilities. I also learned techniques for reducing AI hallucinations and improving the reliability and accuracy of AI-generated outputs in real-world applications.


### Event Experience

What impressed me the most during the event was Team Plan V and their Solution Architect Professional Native App project. The application was designed to assist Solutions Architects in analyzing customer requirements, recommending cloud architectures, generating AWS architecture diagrams, and estimating deployment costs based solely on natural language descriptions. I was particularly impressed by how the team combined Agentic AI with AWS services to automate tasks that traditionally require significant time, expertise, and experience from a Solutions Architect. Their presentation demonstrated that AI is not only a programming assistant but can also serve as a powerful companion for system analysis, solution design, and cloud architecture consulting.

In addition, the presentations from OneTeam, Signal Scout, 3KA, and Six Pillars provided valuable real-world insights into how AI Native solutions can be built on AWS to address challenges across various domains, including business, intelligent monitoring, data analytics, and financial services. Each project clearly demonstrated the complete development process, from problem identification and solution architecture design to selecting appropriate AWS services, deployment, and product demonstration.

Through this event, I not only expanded my knowledge of Cloud Computing, Agentic AI, and system architecture, but also gained valuable product development insights from the participating teams. Their practical experiences inspired me to continue strengthening my AWS skills and further develop my ability to design scalable cloud solutions and AI-powered applications.
